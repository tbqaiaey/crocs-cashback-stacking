## Where cashback refuses to apply at all

These exclusions are the reason most "why didn't I get my cashback" complaints happen, and none of them are negotiable:

- **Collaboration collections.** Crocs collabs are excluded outright. If you're buying a licensed or designer drop, shop it on price alone.
- **International Crocs sites.** The ShopBack US offer covers the Crocs US site. Buying from another country's Crocs storefront earns nothing.
- **Gift cards, vouchers and store credit.** Buying them earns no cashback, and paying with a redeemed gift card or voucher also removes the order from eligibility.
- **Returned or cancelled orders.** Full refunds kill the cashback; partial returns get it prorated down.
- **Final sale and personalised items** can fall under category exceptions.
- **Codes not featured on ShopBack.** Worth repeating a third time, because it's the exclusion that catches people who did everything else right.

There's also the practical matter of how you pay. Major cards, digital wallets and Crocs gift cards are all accepted — but a gift-card-funded order is excluded, so paying by card is the route that keeps cashback alive.
